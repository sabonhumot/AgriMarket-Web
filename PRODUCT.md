# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Buyers in Cebu, Philippines, looking for nearby local produce; approved farmers who list available harvests.

## Product Purpose

AgriMarket helps buyers discover nearby harvests, reserve what is available, and coordinate pickup and payment directly with farmers.

## Operating Context

Buyers browse active crop listings and submit reservation requests. Farmers and buyers use direct messages to arrange fulfillment details.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The public site promotes an Android APK. Listings are from approved farmers across Cebu.

## Brand Commitments

Use the AgriMarket name and preserve its locally connected, practical voice.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the public landing-page copy, AgriMarket logo, and Android app icon. No independent customer testimonials, performance claims, or pricing evidence are provided.

## Product Principles

- Make local harvests easy to discover.
- Keep buyer-to-farmer coordination direct.
- Explain Android installation clearly.
